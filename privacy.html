This policy explains how [COMPANY / TRADING NAME] ("TapDish", "we") looks after your personal information, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Order details: what you ordered, from which restaurant, when, the amount and its status.
  • Notification token: only if you turn on Order alerts. It lets us send a notification to your phone.
  • Your location: only if you tap "Use my location". It stays on your phone to sort restaurants by distance. We do not store it on our servers.
  • Settings and favourites: saved on your own device.

We do not collect or store your card or bank details. Payments are handled by our payment provider.

2. Why we use it

  • To take, process and track your orders.
  • To tell you when your order is ready.
  • To give you support and handle refunds.
  • To keep TapDish secure and prevent fraud.

3. Who we share it with

  • The restaurant you order from sees your name, phone number and order so it can prepare it and match it at pickup.
  • Service providers that run TapDish for us: Google Firebase (database, sign-in, notifications), Vercel (website hosting), Render (servers) and our payment provider [PAYMENT PROVIDER NAME].
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your information.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and order records for as long as you have an account and as long as the law requires us to keep financial records, [e.g. 5 years]. You can ask us to delete your account.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to how we use it. Contact [SUPPORT EMAIL]. You can also change your name and phone number yourself under Profile.

6. Security

We use reputable providers and sensible safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

7. Children

TapDish is not meant for children under 18 without a parent or guardian.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforeg.org.za.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will tell you in the app if the change is important.

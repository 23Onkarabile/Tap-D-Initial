When you get a refund

  • The restaurant rejects or cancels your order after you paid.
  • You were charged but your order was not created.
  • Your order has the wrong or missing items and you tell us within [24 hours] with your order number.

When we cannot refund

  • You did not collect your order.
  • You changed your mind after the restaurant started preparing it.
  • You gave a wrong phone number or name that stopped you collecting the order.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. They usually arrive within [5 to 10 business days], depending on your bank.

How to ask for a refund

Open Profile, then Refunds, then tap Ask about a refund. Or contact contact.tapdish@gmail.com/067 803 5636 with your order number (it starts with GRB).

Your legal rights

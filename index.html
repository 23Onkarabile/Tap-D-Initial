FAQs
How does ordering work?

Choose a restaurant, add what you want, pay online and place your order. The restaurant prepares it, we tell you when it is ready, and you collect it at the restaurant.

Do you deliver?

No. TapDish is pickup only. You order ahead and collect your food yourself.

How will I know my order is ready?

Turn on Order alerts in your profile and we will send a notification the moment the restaurant marks your order ready. You can also open the Orders tab to see its status.

Can I cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number. If the restaurant has not started preparing it, we will do our best to help. See the refund policy for details.

What if the restaurant rejects my order?

You will see the status Rejected. If you already paid, you are refunded. See the refund policy for how and when.

Is my payment safe?

You pay on a secure payment page. TapDish never sees or stores your card or bank details.

Why can't I see restaurants near me?

Allow location when asked, or open Profile and set Show restaurants within to Anywhere. A restaurant may also not have joined TapDish in your area yet.

How do I change my name or phone number?

Open Profile, then Account details or Contact details.

I did not get a notification

Check that Order alerts is on in your profile and that notifications are allowed for TapDish in your browser settings. On iPhone, add TapDish to your Home Screen first.
